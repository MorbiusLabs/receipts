# Singapore's central bank will test AI agents paying with stablecoins. No protocol yet, no start date, and its advice today: don't give agents your bank login

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-24-mas-know-your-agent-sandbox/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-24 · **Status:** all claims sourced to the speech text on mas.gov.sg

On Nov 19 Chia Der Jiun, Managing Director of the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS), gave the keynote at the Singapore FinTech Festival. Headlines since then say Singapore "wants to test stablecoin payments by AI agents" and is rolling out "Know Your Agent". The speech announces both as plans.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MAS will test AI agents making payments with stablecoins | **REAL** | [MAS keynote](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/speeches/2026/keynote-address-by-md-at-the-singapore-fintech-festival-2026), Nov 19, 2026: "We will also set up a Sandbox with the Future of Finance Institute." In its payments use case, banks and payment firms "allow customer agents to execute payment instructions using digital money such as tokenised deposits or stablecoins." |
| 2 | The sandbox is already running | **FALSE** | Same speech: "We will also set up a Sandbox". The speech gives no start date and names no participants |
| 3 | Singapore has a Know Your Agent rule | **FALSE** | Same speech: "we will facilitate the development of know your agent protocol for trusted information exchange between customer agents and financial institutions." It is a protocol to be developed, not a rule |
| 4 | The protocol would tell a bank who the agent is and what it may do | **REAL** | Same speech: "Who the agent is and whom it represents", "What authority it has been given", "What mandate it operates under" and "What assurance exists about its provenance and reliability." |
| 5 | MAS says not to give AI agents your bank login | **REAL** | Same speech: "Some users are giving agents their login credentials to financial accounts. I would not advise giving personal agents such access, as there is insufficient assurance of safety." |
| 6 | MAS has settled who pays when an agent gets it wrong | **FALSE** | Same speech asks it as an open question: "And if agents transact incorrectly or beyond their instruction, who bears responsibility?" |
| 7 | US banks already answer that question in their contracts | **OUR READ** | Wells Fargo's agreement in effect from Nov 24 says agent actions are "deemed to have been initiated and authorized directly by you" ([our check](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-21-wells-fargo-ai-agent-terms/)). Citi's says it "will consider any access and actions taken by the agent as authorized by You" ([our check](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-19-citi-ai-agent-authorized/)) |
