# Nvidia's first quarter without Jensen Huang: a record, and his name nowhere in the release

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-18-nvidia-q3-without-huang/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-18 · **Status:** all claims sourced

Nvidia reported fiscal Q3 2027 (quarter ended Oct 25, 2026) on Nov 17, six days after its board accepted Jensen Huang's resignation. We read the 8-K and both exhibits ourselves.

Primary source: Nvidia 8-K filed Nov 17, 2026, [Ex. 99.1 press release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000088/q3fy27pr.htm) and [Ex. 99.2 CFO commentary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000088/q3fy27cfocommentary.htm).

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revenue was a record $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year ago | **REAL** | Ex. 99.1: "Revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year ago". Ex. 99.2: "a record $115.8 billion" |
| 2 | Data Center revenue was $107.6 billion, up 110% | **REAL** | Ex. 99.1: "Data Center revenue of $107.6 billion, up 110% from a year ago" |
| 3 | Earnings per share were $2.70 GAAP and $2.68 non-GAAP | **REAL** | Ex. 99.1. Since Q1 FY27, non-GAAP no longer excludes stock-based compensation |
| 4 | Next quarter's revenue guide is $130.0 billion, plus or minus 3%, with an extra week and no China data center compute assumed | **REAL** | Ex. 99.1, Outlook: the quarter "includes an additional week" and "NVIDIA is not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China in its outlook" |
| 5 | Gross margin is guided down to 71% to 72%, from 74.9% this quarter | **REAL** | Ex. 99.1: "expected to be between 71.0% and 72.0%". Ex. 99.2 ties the slight Q/Q dip to "the initial Rubin ramp" |
| 6 | The results release never mentions Jensen Huang | **REAL** | We searched the full text of the 8-K, Ex. 99.1 and Ex. 99.2 for "Huang" and "Jensen": 0 matches. The only executive quote is from interim CEO Colette Kress |
| 7 | The filing names the CEO search and the board's independent investigation as new risks | **REAL** | Ex. 99.1 forward-looking text: "the search for a new chief executive officer" and "the ongoing investigation by an independent committee of NVIDIA's board of directors" |
| 8 | The board does not intend to bring Huang back as CEO | **REAL (their reporting)** | Board chair Harvey Jones, quoted by [Reuters](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/nvidia-board-forms-committee-national-102208048.html), Nov 17: "does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO". Not in any Nvidia release we opened |
| 9 | Huang would be "honored" to return | **WATCH** | Reuters paraphrase, with the single word quoted. We have not found Huang's full statement |
| 10 | Nvidia expanded Anthropic's access to its newest chips | **REAL** | Ex. 99.1: "Expanded Anthropic's access to NVIDIA Grace Blackwell and Vera Rubin capacity through cloud providers" |
| 11 | The SEC is investigating Nvidia's disclosures | **NOT CLAIMED** | Reported by the WSJ, cited by Reuters. We have not read the WSJ piece and the SEC declined to comment, so we do not repeat it as fact |
| 12 | Nvidia beat analyst estimates | **NOT CLAIMED** | The consensus figures we saw name no data provider |

## What would change this page

Huang's own statement in full, the 10-Q (due "in the coming weeks", per Ex. 99.2), or an SEC or Nvidia confirmation of the reported probe.

## Corrections

None so far. If you find an error, open an issue with the source.

Update 2026-11-24: on Nov 22 the board reinstated Huang ([our check](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-24-nvidia-huang-reinstated/)). Claim 8 was accurate when reported on Nov 17.
