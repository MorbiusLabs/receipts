# Six senators ask Treasury to protect state stablecoin issuers the way it protects federal applicants

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-22-senators-state-stablecoin-safe-harbor/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-22 · **Status:** the letter itself did not open for us (the senators' sites block access from our location); every claim cites The Block, which quotes the letter

A bipartisan group of senators wrote to Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent about how the GENIUS Act, the US stablecoin law, is being put into practice.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Six senators from both parties wrote to Bessent | **REAL (their reporting)** | [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-11-18-senators-treasury-must-ensure-equal-footing-for-stablecoin-issuers-in-genius-act-implementation-443501), Nov 18: Sens. Cynthia Lummis (R-Wyo.), Kirsten Gillibrand (D-N.Y.), Angela Alsobrooks (D-Md.), Pete Ricketts (R-Neb.), Kevin Cramer (R-N.D.) and Mark Warner (D-Va.). Three Republicans, three Democrats |
| 2 | Treasury's draft gives a safe harbor to issuers waiting on federal regulators but not to state issuers | **REAL (their reporting)** | The letter, as quoted: "Treasury's current proposal would offer stablecoin issuers with pending applications to federal regulators a safe harbor as the GENIUS Act requirements come into effect, if such a regulator grants a waiver, without a corresponding protection for state issuers" |
| 3 | They want issuers free to pick a state or a federal charter | **REAL (their reporting)** | "it must allow issuers to choose from state or federal charters, as contemplated by the GENIUS Act, without the risk of business disruption or uncertainty" |
| 4 | States have until January 2028 to certify their rules | **REAL (their reporting)** | The Block: the law "includes language about a January 2028 deadline where states must submit certifications" that their rules are "substantially similar" to federal ones. State rules apply to issuers with $10 billion or less |
| 5 | Treasury has agreed | **NOT CLAIMED** | No Treasury response appears in anything we read |
