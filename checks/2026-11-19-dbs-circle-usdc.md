# DBS lets institutions mint and redeem USDC directly, around the clock

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-19-dbs-circle-usdc/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-19 · **Status:** all claims sourced

Southeast Asia's largest bank by assets and the issuer of USDC announced a partnership on Nov 18. It is for institutions, not for retail customers.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Eligible institutional clients can mint and redeem USDC directly through DBS | **REAL** | [DBS release](https://www.dbs.com/newsroom/DBS_and_Circle_expand_institutional_access_to_USDC_in_Asia), Nov 18: "eligible institutional clients can directly mint and redeem USDC through DBS" |
| 2 | USD to USDC settlement runs 24/7 | **REAL** | Same release: "24/7 USD-USDC settlement when minting and redeeming the stablecoin through DBS" |
| 3 | Any DBS customer can now buy USDC from the bank | **NOT CLAIMED** | Only "eligible institutional clients". The release names no markets and no eligibility rules |
| 4 | 88% of large corporations are deploying or piloting tokenised money | **REAL (their figure)** | Same release, citing DBS's own report "The Great Rewiring". A survey by the bank, not an independent count |
| 5 | Both firms take part in Singapore's Project BLOOM | **REAL** | Same release: an initiative led by the Monetary Authority of Singapore |
