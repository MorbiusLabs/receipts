# Payoneer's stablecoin bank got a preliminary OCC approval. It cannot open or issue PAYO-USD until a final one

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-24-payoneer-occ-conditional-stablecoin-bank/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-24 · **Status:** all claims sourced to Payoneer's own press release; the OCC decision letter did not open for us

On Nov 20 Payoneer said the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) gave it "preliminary conditional approval" to form PAYO Digital Bank, a national trust bank for stablecoins. Headlines shortened that to "Payoneer wins OCC approval for a stablecoin trust bank". Payoneer's own release says what the approval does and does not allow yet.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The OCC approved Payoneer's plan for a stablecoin trust bank | **REAL (their claim)** | [Payoneer press release](https://www.payoneer.com/press/payoneer-receives-occ-conditional-approval-to-form-stablecoin-national-trust-bank/), Nov 20, 2026: Payoneer "has received preliminary conditional approval from the U.S. Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) to form PAYO Digital Bank, a national trust bank designed to support stablecoin functionality for global business" |
| 2 | PAYO Digital Bank is open for business | **FALSE** | Same release: "PAYO Digital Bank is now able to operate in its organizational phase, while it seeks final approval to open for business." |
| 3 | Payoneer has launched its stablecoin PAYO-USD | **FALSE** | Same release: "Once PAYO Digital Bank receives final OCC approval to open and issue payment stablecoins, it plans to issue PAYO-USD, a fully reserved, GENIUS Act-compliant stablecoin." |
| 4 | PAYO-USD will trade on exchanges | **FALSE** | Same release: "PAYO-USD, which will not be listed on exchanges, will serve as a balance within the Payoneer ecosystem" |
| 5 | A third of Payoneer's customers already use stablecoins | **REAL (their claim)** | Rob Morgan, CEO of PAYO Digital Bank, in the release: "More than one third of our customers already use stablecoin today." Payoneer gives no count or definition |
| 6 | The bank will let businesses hold stablecoins and convert them to local money | **REAL (their plan)** | Same release: customers will "hold stablecoin balances in bank-approved digital wallets", "pay or be paid in a variety of third-party stablecoins, and convert their balances into fiat in the local markets where they operate" |

The application was filed in February ([Payoneer, Feb 24, 2026](https://www.payoneer.com/press/payoneer-files-application-for-u-s-national-trust-bank-charter-to-strengthen-regulated-financial-infrastructure-for-global-businesses)). Reports also give the conditions in the OCC letter, such as deadlines to raise capital and to open. We did not open that letter, so we do not repeat them here.

## What would change this page
