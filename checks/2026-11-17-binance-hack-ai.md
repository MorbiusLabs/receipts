# Did AI hack Binance? What Binance's own report says

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-17-binance-hack-ai/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-17 · **Status:** all claims sourced

Hackers took about $6.3 billion from Binance on Oct 29. The theft is often described as an AI hack. We read what Binance said about how it happened. We could not open Binance's own post, so every claim below cites two outlets that quote its incident report.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hackers drained about $6.3B in crypto from Binance on Oct 29 | **REAL (their reporting)** | Binance, as quoted by [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/markets/binance-hack-fake-job-interview-heist-rcna607238): "$6.3 billion worth of crypto". [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-03-binance-hack-incident-report-north-korea-fake-job-interview-ai-tool-432406) gives $6.34B |
| 2 | The break-in started in June, when a wallet engineer installed an AI coding tool from a tampered download | **REAL (their reporting)** | The Block: "a tampered Homebrew installation downloaded while he was trying to install an AI coding tool". NBC: an employee "downloaded an AI tool that had been tampered with" |
| 3 | In September a second engineer was targeted with a fake job interview | **REAL (their reporting)** | The Block: "a fraudulent recruitment process, posing as company executives" |
| 4 | On Oct 20 the attackers used a compromised computer and an internal AI tool to take over two more engineers' devices | **REAL (their reporting)** | The Block: "used the compromised employee's computer and an internal AI tool to gain control of two other engineers' devices" |
| 5 | An AI carried out the attack | **FALSE** | Binance describes "social engineering, malware, and compromised internal AI tooling" used by people (The Block). Nothing we opened says an AI system ran the attack |
| 6 | Binance says it froze or intercepted about $2.85B and replenished 98% of affected assets | **REAL (their reporting)** | The Block, quoting Binance |
| 7 | The FBI linked the attack to North Korea, which rejects the claim | **REAL (their reporting)** | NBC News and The Block |

## What would change this page

Binance's promised independent review, or an FBI statement that gives the AI tools a larger role. If Binance's own post uses different wording from the two outlets, we will update the quotes with the date.

**Update, 2026-11-19:** Google's Nov 17 AI threat report describes a North Korea-linked cluster abusing AI tools inside crypto firms. It does not name Binance, so it does not change the verdicts above. See [our check of that report](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-19-google-ai-hacking-no-human/).
