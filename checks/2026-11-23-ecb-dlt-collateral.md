# The ECB will take tokenised assets from licensed DLT platforms as collateral, from June 2027

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-23-ecb-dlt-collateral/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** all claims sourced to the ECB press release of Nov 23, 2026

The ECB's Governing Council decided on Nov 23 to widen the tokenised assets it accepts as collateral. It is a decision with a start date, not a live change. It follows [our Nov 20 check of Lagarde's speech](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-20-lagarde-tokenised-assets/).

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The ECB will accept assets issued on licensed DLT platforms as collateral | **REAL** | [ECB press release](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2026/html/ecb.pr261123~76b6f57b74.en.html): the Governing Council approved "the acceptance of marketable assets issued on DLT platforms licensed under the EU DLT Pilot Regime Regulation as eligible collateral for Eurosystem credit operations", subject to specific requirements |
| 2 | It starts in June 2027 | **REAL** | The ECB expects to publish the requirements "in the spring of 2027, with implementation planned to take effect in June 2027". A plan, so the date can move |
| 3 | The assets still go through the existing settlement system | **REAL** | They must be made available "via an eligible link to a central securities depository (CSD) operating an eligible securities settlement system", and those systems must "offer settlement in the TARGET2-Securities platform" |
| 4 | It builds on an earlier decision this year | **REAL** | "Today's decision follows the announcement on 27 January 2026 to accept as collateral assets issued in CSDs using DLT-based services." |
| 5 | The ECB now accepts assets that live entirely on a blockchain | **NOT CLAIMED** | The ECB says its roadmap "foresees further exploration of ways to expand eligibility to assets issued and settled entirely on DLT networks". That is still being explored |

Executive Board member Isabel Schnabel: "We support innovation without lowering our standards".

## What would change this page

The spring 2027 requirements, or a change to the June 2027 date.
