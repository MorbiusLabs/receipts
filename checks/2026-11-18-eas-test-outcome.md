# The Nov 17 "blackout" came and went: the alert test ran, the internet stayed on

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-18-eas-test-outcome/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-18 · **Status:** all claims sourced

On Nov 16 we checked a rumor that the internet would shut down for three months on Nov 17, and found that the real event was a nationwide Emergency Alert System test. Here is what happened when the day came.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The nationwide EAS test ran at about 2:20 p.m. ET on Nov 17 | **REAL (their reporting)** | [Radio World](https://www.radioworld.com/news-and-business/headlines/nationwide-eas-test-runs-without-major-hiccups), Nov 17. Some stations aired it a few minutes later |
| 2 | The message lasted 18 seconds, in English and Spanish, on radio, TV, cable, satellite and wireline video | **REAL (their reporting)** | Same article |
| 3 | Initial reports show no major technical problems | **REAL (their reporting)** | Same article: the drill "went off without major technical issues" |
| 4 | FEMA and the FCC will now review the results | **REAL (their reporting)** | FEMA, quoted by Radio World: "Now that the test is complete, FEMA will work with the FCC to analyze the results." Broadcasters' day-of-test reports are due Nov 19, detailed reports by Jan 4, 2027 |
| 5 | All nine blockchains we track were producing blocks after the test | **REAL** | Our [chain-pulse](https://pulse.morbiuslabs.org/) snapshot at 2:49 p.m. ET showed all nine chains live. It was the only snapshot that hour, because GitHub runs our 5-minute job on a best-effort schedule |
| 6 | The internet went down for three months starting Nov 17 | **FALSE** | Nothing of the kind happened. See [our Nov 16 check](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-16-banks-down-internet-off/) for where the rumor came from |
