# Nvidia reports today, its first quarter without Jensen Huang

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-17-nvidia-first-report-without-huang/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-17 · **Status:** all claims sourced

Nvidia reports fiscal third-quarter results after the market closes today. It is the company's first report since its founder stepped down. Everything below comes from Nvidia's own filing with the SEC.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jensen Huang resigned as President, CEO and board member on Nov 12, effective immediately | **REAL** | [Nvidia press release filed with the SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm), Nov 12, 2026: "Jensen Huang has resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of the Board, effective immediately" |
| 2 | CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and stays CFO | **REAL** | Same release: Kress "has agreed to serve as Interim Chief Executive Officer" and "will continue to serve as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer" |
| 3 | The board named Harvey C. Jones its first-ever chair | **REAL** | Same release: "The Board of Directors appointed Harvey C. Jones as its first-ever Chair of the Board" |
| 4 | Results come today at about 1:20 p.m. PT, which is 4:20 p.m. ET | **REAL** | Same release: "on Tuesday, November 17, at approximately 1:20 p.m. PT, as previously scheduled" |
| 5 | Nvidia's stock is down about 24% since Oct 30 | **REAL** | Close $204.64 on Oct 30, $156.14 on Nov 16. See [our IPO check](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-17-anthropic-ipo-draining-crypto/#claim-9) |
| 6 | The resignation caused the drop | **NOT CLAIMED** | Most of the fall came before Nov 12, including the Nov 5 sell-off. We do not link the two |
