# Fed Governor Cook: $2 trillion of data centers announced, "only a fraction" built. AI job losses "could precede" new jobs

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-20-cook-data-centers-jobs/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-20 · **Status:** all claims sourced from the speech

Federal Reserve Governor Lisa Cook spoke on Nov 20 about the US economy in a global context.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Companies have announced more than $2 trillion of data centers | **REAL (her figures)** | [Fed speech](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/cook20261120a.htm): "Companies have announced plans for more than $2 trillion in specific data center construction projects." The figure is footnoted to an outside source |
| 2 | Most of it has been built | **FALSE** | Same passage: "So far, only a fraction of this has been realized." |
| 3 | The AI buildout is raising other prices | **REAL (her analysis)** | She sees "spillovers" in "the price of chips and high-tech equipment, construction wages, and electricity costs" |
| 4 | The Fed says AI will destroy jobs for good | **FALSE** | Cook: "permanent job loss unlikely". Her worry is timing: a "nonzero probability that this transition could take time and that job losses due to AI could precede new job creation" |
| 5 | Some jobs are already feeling it | **REAL (her reading)** | "labor demand has cooled for coding jobs in the software industry and in simultaneous translation". Overall, "it is too soon to feel comforted" |
| 6 | Inflation has been above target for years | **REAL** | "inflation is well above the Fed's 2 percent target and has been for 67 months" |
