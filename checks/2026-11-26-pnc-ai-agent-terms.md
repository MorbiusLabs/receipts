# PNC's new terms let AI agents in only through its own connection, with no passwords and no payments unless PNC allows it. They take effect Dec 19

**Post:** [X, Nov 26](https://x.com/morbius_labs/status/2125874727713452255) and [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-26-pnc-ai-agent-terms/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-26 · **Status:** all claims sourced to PNC's Digital Services Agreement, version effective 12/19/2026 (PDF on pnc.com)

TechCrunch reported on Nov 18 that PNC had changed its terms for AI agents. We have now read the agreement itself. It is the upcoming version, effective Dec 19, 2026, with a new "Third-Party AI Agents" section on pages 8 to 10.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PNC added an AI agent section to its online banking terms | **REAL** | [Digital Services Agreement](https://www.pnc.com/content/dam/digital-assets/en/documents/digital_services_agreement_UPDATE.pdf), "Version Effective 12/19/2026": section "Third-Party AI Agents", pp. 8–10, and a new defined term "Third-Party AI Agent" |
| 2 | The new terms are already in effect | **FALSE** | Same document, cover page: "Version Effective 12/19/2026" |
| 3 | PNC bans AI agents | **FALSE** | Same section: "you may be permitted to connect a Third-Party AI Agent ... through a connection method PNC makes available by authenticating directly with PNC and specifying the Eligible Account(s), information, duration, and permissions" |
| 4 | Agents cannot move your money unless PNC says so | **REAL** | Same section: "Unless PNC expressly permits otherwise for a particular Service, you may not authorize or permit a Third-Party AI Agent to initiate, request, submit, or complete a payment, transfer, or other transaction on your behalf" |
| 5 | You may not give an agent your password or one-time code | **REAL** | Same section: you "must not disclose, share, enter, or otherwise provide your User ID or password ... including a one-time passcode or other security credential, to any Third-Party AI Agent" |
| 6 | Agents that drive a browser or scrape the site are on you | **REAL** | Same section bars "browser automation, screen scraping, an embedded or shared browser session"; then "To the extent permitted by applicable law, you are responsible for access and activities you authorize or permit in violation of these restrictions, including unintended activities or activities exceeding the authority you intended to provide" |
| 7 | PNC is not responsible when an agent gets it wrong | **REAL** | Same section: "Except as otherwise required by applicable law, PNC is not responsible if a Third-Party AI Agent misunderstands or incorrectly follows your instructions, takes an action you did not intend ... or acts outside the access or authority you gave it" |
| 8 | Instructions that pass PNC's security checks bind you even if an agent sent them | **REAL** | Same document, security procedure: you agree to be bound by instructions sent in compliance with it "regardless of whether you, a Delegate, Third-Party AI Agent, or any Authorized Representative actually authorized the instruction". The same sentence carves out "Consumer deposit accounts", which follow PNC's Consumer Electronic Funds Transfer Disclosure Statement |
| 9 | Revoking an agent works the moment you click | **FALSE** | Same section: revocation takes effect "after we receive it and have a reasonable opportunity to act on it", and "you remain responsible for the Third-Party AI Agent's access and activities until the revocation or modification becomes effective" |

## What would change this page

PNC publishing a list of Services where agents may make payments, or a revised version before Dec 19.
