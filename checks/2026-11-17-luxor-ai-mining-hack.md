# Did AI hijack Bitcoin miners? What Luxor actually said

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-17-luxor-ai-mining-hack/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-17 · **Status:** all claims sourced

Headlines say an AI attack hijacked tens of thousands of Bitcoin mining machines through Luxor's Commander software. We separated what Luxor confirmed from what others estimated. We could not open Luxor's own posts on X, so its statements are quoted from Bitcoin.com News.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Luxor detected an unauthorized redirect of hashrate through Commander | **REAL (their reporting)** | Luxor on X, quoted by [Bitcoin.com News](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/luxor-confirms-bitcoin-mining-hack-and-reveals-ai-enabled-attack/), Nov 15: "an unauthorized redirect of hashrate through Commander" |
| 2 | Just over a dozen client accounts were affected, and no data left Luxor's systems | **REAL (their reporting)** | Same statement: "Impact: just over a dozen client accounts." and "No data was leaked or exfiltrated from our systems." |
| 3 | Affected clients will be paid in full for the redirected hashrate | **REAL (their reporting)** | Same statement: "every affected client will be paid in full for the redirected hashrate, as if it had mined normally" |
| 4 | Luxor's CEO says customers were compromised through an AI-enabled method | **REAL (their reporting)** | Nick Hansen, quoted by Bitcoin.com: "a subset of Commander customers were compromised, through a sophisticated AI-enabled vector" |
| 5 | AI carried out the attack | **WATCH** | Only the CEO's statement supports it, and Luxor has not published how the attackers got in. Bitcoin.com notes no technical findings yet |
| 6 | 64,000 machines and 13 EH/s were hijacked | **NOT CLAIMED** | These figures come from third-party estimates, first publicized by CoreX Hosting. Luxor has not confirmed them, so we do not repeat them as fact |

## What would change this page
