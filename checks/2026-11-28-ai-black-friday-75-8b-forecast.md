# No, AI didn't buy $75.8 billion of Black Friday. It's a Salesforce forecast for the whole week, worldwide, and it counts sales "influenced" by AI

**Post:** [X, Nov 28](https://x.com/morbius_labs/status/2126600724008042777) and [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-28-ai-black-friday-75-8b-forecast/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-28 · **Status:** sourced to the Salesforce release of Nov 19 and the Reuters report of Nov 27 (text via The Star); Adobe's final Black Friday total was not yet published

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI drove (or bought) $75.8 billion of Black Friday sales | **FALSE** | The figure is a forecast for all of Cyber Week, worldwide, of sales "influenced by AI and agents". See claims 2 to 5 |
| 2 | Salesforce put $75.8 billion on AI | **REAL** | [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/cyber-week-ai-sales-2026/), Nov 19: "$75.8 billion of that figure will be influenced by AI and agents, up 13% from 2025" |
| 3 | The figure is a forecast, made before Black Friday | **REAL** | Same release: "Salesforce today predicts that the 2026 Cyber Week (Tuesday, November 24, through Monday, November 30) will drive a record $357 billion in global online sales" |
| 4 | It covers one day in the US | **FALSE** | Same release: seven days, global. "$75.8 billion, or 21% of all global sales". The US forecast for the week is $84 billion in total |
| 5 | "Influenced" means an AI agent placed the order | **NOT CLAIMED** | The release does not define "influenced". Methodology: "proprietary Salesforce research … first-party and third-party data, as well as several market assumptions" |
| 6 | US shoppers spent $9.7 billion online on Black Friday by evening | **REAL (their reporting)** | [Reuters](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/11/28/us-black-friday-online-sales-to-cross-13-billion-adobe-says), Nov 27, Adobe Analytics: "$9.7 billion through 6:30 p.m. ET (2330 GMT), up 12.8% from last year" |
| 7 | Black Friday online spending set a record above $13 billion | **WATCH** | Same article: "The final total is expected to land between $13 billion and $13.4 billion". Last year: $11.8 billion. Final figure not published when we checked |
| 8 | Salesforce counted $5.9 billion in the US in the first half of the day | **REAL (their reporting)** | Same article: "$5.9 billion in the US and $30.7 billion globally in the first half of the day" |
| 9 | AI traffic to shops is growing fast | **REAL (their reporting)** | Salesforce, Nov 19: "AI-driven traffic to retailer websites is up 104% year over year" and AI-referred shoppers are "two times as likely to convert". Salesforce's own data, not independent |

## What would change this page

Salesforce publishing its actual Cyber Week results with a definition of "influenced", or a measured count of orders placed by AI agents.
