# Google saw AI find and exploit bugs with no human involved. It does not mention Binance

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-19-google-ai-hacking-no-human/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-19 · **Status:** all claims sourced

Google's threat intelligence team published its quarterly AI threat report on Nov 17. Coverage put it next to the Binance theft. The report itself does not.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Attackers used AI to find and exploit bugs with no human in the loop, a first | **REAL (their analysis)** | [Google Threat Intelligence Group](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/agentic-threat-activity-crosses-a-significant-threshold), Nov 17: threat actors used LLM frameworks "to fully automate vulnerability discovery and exploitation without a human in the loop for the first time" |
| 2 | The tool's files had Russian comments | **REAL (their analysis)** | Same report shows Russian-language comments in the framework, called exploit_agent, with English translations. Google does not attribute the tool to the Russian state |
| 3 | The tool targeted crypto firms | **NOT CLAIMED** | Same report: the operator was interested in "French websites associated with the media and entertainment industries, as well as the education sector" |
| 4 | North Korea-linked hackers abused AI tools inside crypto firms | **REAL (their analysis)** | Same report: a suspected MIDNIGHT NEPTUNE cluster, in "intrusions targeting cryptocurrency organizations in North America and Europe", exploited LLM software "as a lateral execution surface". It sits under the report's DPRK section |
| 5 | Google linked these intrusions to the Binance theft | **NOT CLAIMED** | The report does not mention Binance. [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/11/18/google-finds-self-running-ai-hacking-tool-with-russian-comments-in-its-files) put the two side by side. See our [Binance check](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-17-binance-hack-ai/) |
| 6 | Fake recruiters used AI-made profile images | **REAL (their analysis)** | Same report: recruiter profiles whose images "contained SynthID watermarks, confirming use of LLMs" |
