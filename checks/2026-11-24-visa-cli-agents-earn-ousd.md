# Visa's agent wallet lets AI agents earn a stablecoin for tasks. It is invite-only, in sandbox, and pay is not guaranteed

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-24-visa-cli-agents-earn-ousd/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-24 · **Status:** all claims sourced to Visa CLI's own documentation

On Nov 23 crypto media reported that Visa launched an experimental wallet that lets AI agents earn OUSD, a dollar stablecoin, for paid tasks. Visa CLI's documentation confirms the setup and is careful about what it does not promise.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI agents can take paid tasks and get paid in a stablecoin | **REAL** | [Visa CLI docs, tasks](https://app.visacli.sh/docs/tasks-and-earning): "When the requester accepts, the reward is paid to your wallet." [Card page](https://app.visacli.sh/docs/your-visa-card): the agent "pays in OUSD on Tempo, from your wallet" |
| 2 | It is built by Visa | **REAL** | [Status page](https://app.visacli.sh/docs/status): "Visa CLI is built by Visa Crypto Labs." The [docs](https://app.visacli.sh/docs) call it "a Visa product, in sandbox today" |
| 3 | Your Visa card gets charged | **FALSE** | Card page: "Adding the card doesn't charge it, and your agent doesn't pay with it: it pays in OUSD on Tempo, from your wallet." |
| 4 | The card check is an identity check like KYC | **FALSE** | Card page: "It isn't a KYC, KYB or sanctions check." It ties agents to one person: "up to 25 agents at the moment" per card |
| 5 | Anyone in the US can use it now | **FALSE** | Status page: "Accounts are approved by invitation". Card page: "Visa CLI is US-only today." Docs: "Prepaid, corporate and JPMorgan Chase cards can't be added yet." |
| 6 | An agent can spend on its own from the start | **FALSE** | [Limits page](https://app.visacli.sh/docs/limits-and-approvals): "Off for a new agent: every payment waits for your approval." If you turn it on, "payments under this amount, fees included, go through alone", and "Visa may also cap what all your agents spend together in a day" |
| 7 | A finished task means you get paid | **FALSE** | Tasks page: "A completed task alone is not proof that a reward was paid." The requester has to accept the work first |

## What would change this page

Visa publishing payout or task data, the docs dropping "sandbox", or a Visa newsroom release.
