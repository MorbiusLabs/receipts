# SBI Solana Global is live. The "$2.3T" is Solana's line about the parent groups, not a figure in the joint release

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-25-sbi-solana-global-2-3t/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-25 · **Status:** sourced to the joint release (SBI Holdings PDF) and Solana's post on X; we did not check the $2.3T sum

On Nov 25 SBI Holdings, Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group (SMFG) and the Solana Foundation announced SBI Solana Global. Posts said the venture brings "$2.3T+" to on-chain finance. The joint release gives no money figure at all.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SBI, SMFG and the Solana Foundation launched SBI Solana Global | **REAL** | [Joint release](https://www.sbigroup.co.jp/english/news/pdf/2026/1125_a_en.pdf), Nov 25, 2026: the three "have completed a series of agreements for the transition to a new joint venture structure", and "SBI R3 Japan Co., Ltd. has commenced full-scale operations as “SBI Solana Global Co., Ltd.”" |
| 2 | It is a brand-new company | **FALSE** | Same release: it is SBI R3 Japan Co., Ltd. under a new name and a new three-party structure |
| 3 | The joint release says $2.3 trillion | **FALSE** | The release names no assets, capital, stake or customer count. The figure is from [Solana's post on X](https://x.com/solana/status/2125515694645321742), Nov 25: "SBI Holdings and SMFG bring $2.3T in combined assets and 80M+ customers to Japan's onchain financial market" |
| 4 | $2.3 trillion is going into the venture | **OUR READ** | Solana's post describes the parent groups' combined assets. The release gives no capital or investment figure for SBI Solana Global, so nothing says how much money the venture itself has |
| 5 | AI agents already make micropayments on it | **FALSE** | Release: payment rails "while also addressing emerging payment use cases such as micropayments conducted by AI agents". A planned use case, not a live service |
| 6 | Products start on set dates | **NOT CLAIMED** | The release gives no launch dates for any of the four business areas |
| 7 | Japan comes first, then Asia | **REAL** | Release: the three parties "will first establish practical use cases and a solid business foundation in Japan, and then extend the resulting expertise across Asia" |

## What would change this page

A filing or release giving SBI Solana Global's capital or ownership split, or a dated product launch.
