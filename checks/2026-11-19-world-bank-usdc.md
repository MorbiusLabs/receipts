# The World Bank will explore paying in USDC with Citi and Circle. Not a pilot yet

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-19-world-bank-usdc/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-19 · **Status:** all claims sourced

On Nov 17 the World Bank Group said it would look at regulated stablecoins for moving development money. Some coverage called it a test. The release uses one verb throughout: explore.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The World Bank Group will explore a stablecoin-based payment model | **REAL** | [World Bank Group release](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/11/17/world-bank-group-explores-regulated-stablecoins-to-modernize-payments-redirect-res), Nov 17: an initial exploration of an intermediated, interoperable stablecoin-based payment model |
| 2 | Circle would mint and settle in USDC; Citi brings the banking rails | **REAL** | Same release. Citi says its network spans more than 180 jurisdictions |
| 3 | It covers borrower countries where digital assets are permitted | **REAL** | Same release. No country is named in the text |
| 4 | The World Bank is already paying in stablecoins, or has launched a pilot | **NOT CLAIMED** | The release gives no amount, no start date and no first payment |
| 5 | The World Bank already runs a blockchain platform for disbursements | **REAL (their figure)** | Same release: FundsChain, with more than 350 projects across 96 countries onboarded |
| 6 | The aim is cheaper, faster, more traceable payments than correspondent banking | **REAL (their words)** | Same release. President Ajay Banga: the goal is to "make the financial plumbing of development work better for the people who need it most" |

## What would change it

A named country, a start date or a first payment would move this from exploring to a pilot. We will add it here.
