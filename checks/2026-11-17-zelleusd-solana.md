# Zelle sent $1.25M in ZelleUSD on Solana. When does it launch?

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-17-zelleusd-solana/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-17 · **Status:** all claims sourced

Zelle, the bank-owned payment app used by millions of Americans, showed its own dollar stablecoin on stage at Solana's Breakpoint conference. We checked what was actually shown and what is still missing.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Zelle sent $1.25M in ZelleUSD (ZLUSD) in a confidential transfer on Solana, live on stage at Breakpoint | **REAL (their reporting)** | [Solana's X account](https://x.com/solana/status/2122625793641816300): Zelle "executed its first confidential bank transfer on Solana, sending $1.25M in ZelleUSD ($ZLUSD) live on stage at Breakpoint" |
| 2 | ZelleUSD will launch on Solana for international person-to-person payments, issued by Anchorage Digital Bank | **REAL (their reporting)** | [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/zelle-solana-zelleusd-stablecoin-international-payments), Nov 16, quoting Zelle executives on stage: "ZelleUSD's issuer is Anchorage Digital Bank" |
| 3 | Zelle's Alexandra Lang: "Solana is a partner we chose because we're here to deliver payments at scale." | **REAL (their reporting)** | Same Cointelegraph article |
| 4 | No launch date was given | **REAL (their reporting)** | Cointelegraph: Zelle "did not provide a timeline for the launch of international payments on stage" |
| 5 | Zelle's own newsroom had no release about it | **REAL** | [zelle.com/newsroom](https://www.zelle.com/newsroom), checked Nov 17, 2026 |
| 6 | ZelleUSD goes live for customers | **WATCH** | Shown on stage, not launched. We will update this page when Zelle or Early Warning announces a date |
