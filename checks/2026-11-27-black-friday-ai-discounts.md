# This Black Friday, AI is on both sides of the checkout: brands use it to trim discounts, shoppers get agents that can buy. No sign of a personal price for you

**Post:** [X, Nov 27](https://x.com/morbius_labs/status/2126254017785323762) and [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-27-black-friday-ai-discounts/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-27 · **Status:** sourced to the Reuters report of Nov 23 (text via Investing.com) and the ACCC media release 128/26 of Nov 27; vendor case studies name no clients

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Brands use AI pricing tools to cut discounts | **REAL (their reporting)** | [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/ai-tools-help-brands-curb-black-friday-deals-from-clothes-to-skincare-5044922), Nov 23, from "interviews with five firms that provide AI tools": "Often, the tools conclude that companies can reduce discounts without dampening demand" |
| 2 | Gap uses AI for markdowns | **REAL** | Same article. A Gap spokesperson said the brand uses Impact Analytics to make "more informed markdown decisions based on demand and inventory signals" |
| 3 | An AI tool told a beauty brand to cut a skincare deal from 40% to 30% | **REAL (their reporting)** | Same article: Kapnova's tools "advised a billion-dollar beauty brand to reduce this year's Black Friday discount on a skincare product to 30% from an initially planned 40%", per CEO James Sun. The client is not named |
| 4 | A buy-one-get-one deal was cut from 50% to 40% | **REAL (their reporting)** | Same article, per First Insight founder Greg Petro. The client is not named |
| 5 | Under Armour, Tiffany, Dolce & Gabbana and YSL cut their Black Friday discounts | **NOT CLAIMED** | The vendors say these brands use their pricing software. Reuters reports they "did not respond", and nothing shows they cut a specific discount |
| 6 | Average discounts are getting smaller | **REAL (their reporting)** | Same article, Centric Market Intelligence data: the average on-sale sports bra was discounted "by 32%, against 36% the year before". Reuters adds that "the full scale of its price impact is unknown" |
| 7 | AI sets a personal price just for you | **FALSE** | Same article: "Predictive algorithms typically stop short of pricing items differently for individual customers". Walmart and Target say they don't do it |
| 8 | AI agents can now shop for you | **REAL** | [ACCC media release 128/26](https://www.accc.gov.au/media-release/think-and-check-before-letting-ai-shop-on-your-behalf), Nov 27: "newer AI products are able to make purchases". It names Meta's Muse, OpenAI's ChatGPT dots and Google Tasks |
| 9 | Agents can show you scams | **REAL** | Same release: agents "may present a scam product or service alongside legitimate products or services" |
| 10 | Using an AI agent changes your consumer rights | **FALSE** | Same release, for Australia: "their consumer guarantee rights under Australian Consumer Law do not change" |

## What would change this page

A brand confirming on the record that an AI tool cut a specific Black Friday discount, or data showing personalised prices.
