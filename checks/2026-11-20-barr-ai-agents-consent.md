# "AI can access your bank account without permission"? A Fed governor said banks can't verify consent

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-20-barr-ai-agents-consent/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-20 · **Status:** all claims sourced from the speech; the Townhall article behind the headline did not open for us

A headline on Nov 18 read "Fed Governor Warns That AI Can Access Your Bank Account Without Permission". We could not open that article, so we checked the claim against the speech it most likely draws on: Governor Michael Barr's Nov 16 speech in Milan, which we already [checked for its bank-run part](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-19-barr-ai-bank-runs/).

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Big banks are finding AI agents trying to act in customers' accounts | **REAL** | [Barr, Nov 16](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/barr20261116a.htm): "major banks now report finding AI agents attempting to take actions in retail customer accounts" |
| 2 | The banks cannot tell whether the customer agreed | **REAL** | Same sentence: "without the bank being able to verify authenticity, consent, and alignment with customer instructions" |
| 3 | AI can get into your bank account without your permission | **NOT CLAIMED** | The speech describes a verification gap: the bank cannot confirm consent. It does not say the agents acted without the owner's permission. The words "permission", "authorized" and "unauthorized" do not appear in the speech |
| 4 | Barr named the banks, or Citi | **NOT CLAIMED** | No bank is named. Citi's new rule that third-party agent actions count as "authorized by You" is a [separate story](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-19-citi-ai-agent-authorized/); no source we read links the two |
| 5 | He proposed fixes | **REAL** | Guardrails he lists: "strict authentication, explicit consent verification for specific actions, technical controls limiting agent capabilities" |
