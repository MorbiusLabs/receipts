# "The SEC is probing Nvidia's accounting": reported, not confirmed

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-20-nvidia-sec-probe-report/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-20 · **Status:** the probe rests on anonymous sources; no filing or statement confirms it

On Nov 20 Reuters reported, citing two people briefed on the matter, that Nvidia's accounting is the focus of an SEC investigation. Nvidia stock closed down 14% that day.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The SEC is investigating Nvidia's accounting | **WATCH** | Reuters (via [Korea Times reprint](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/world/20261121/nvidias-accounting-is-focus-of-us-sec-investigation-sources-say)), citing "two people briefed on the matter". "A spokesperson for the SEC declined to comment." |
| 2 | Nvidia has confirmed it | **FALSE** | Reuters: "Spokespeople for Nvidia did not immediately respond to requests for comment." Nvidia's [EDGAR filings](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001045810) show no 8-K about an inquiry. The latest 8-Ks are Nov 12 (CEO change) and Nov 17 (results) |
| 3 | We know what the SEC is looking at | **FALSE** | Reuters "could not immediately determine when the SEC opened its inquiry or exactly what potential accounting issues the watchdog is examining." The Wall Street Journal, which first reported a probe on Nov 17, also could not say what it covers, per Reuters |
| 4 | Nvidia stopped showing Singapore separately in its revenue breakdown | **REAL (their reporting)** | Reuters, citing the Financial Times: the change "resulted in Singapore being removed from its geographical breakdown". Reuters presents this as press scrutiny, not as a confirmed focus of the SEC |
| 5 | The probe is why Jensen Huang was removed | **NOT CLAIMED** | No source we read makes that link. See [our Nvidia story](https://morbiuslabs.org/stories/nvidia-without-huang/) |
| 6 | Nvidia fell about 10% on Nov 20 | **REAL** | It fell more by the close. [Nasdaq historical data](https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity/stocks/nvda/historical): $125.53 against $145.98, down 14.0%, on 633 million shares, about twice the day before. Down 19.3% from the Nov 17 close, the day of its results. During the session [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/stocks-moving-the-most-today-live-updates.html) had it "heading for its worst session since Jan. 27, 2025". No source measures how much of the drop came from the Reuters report |

## What would change this page

An Nvidia 8-K or 10-Q disclosure, an SEC statement, or a denial.

## Corrections

2026-11-21: claim 6 updated from the intraday figure (about 10%) to Nasdaq's close (down 14.0%).
