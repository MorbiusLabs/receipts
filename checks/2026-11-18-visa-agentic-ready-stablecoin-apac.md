# Visa signs up 50+ Asia-Pacific payment firms to test AI-agent checkout, and pilots stablecoin-funded payouts

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-18-visa-agentic-ready-stablecoin-apac/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-18 · **Status:** all claims sourced

Visa put out two Asia-Pacific releases on Nov 18: one on AI agents paying at merchants, one on stablecoins inside Visa Direct. Both are real steps. Neither means your AI assistant can pay at these shops yet, and neither pays anyone in stablecoins.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Visa's Agentic Ready program now covers the merchant side in Asia Pacific, starting with 50+ participants | **REAL** | [Visa release](https://www.visa.com.sg/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases/visa-expands-agentic-ready-to-help-scale-trusted-ai-commerce-in-asia-pacific.html), Nov 18: acquirers, payment providers, gateways and selected merchants, "launching with 50+ participants", more "throughout 2027" |
| 2 | Named partners include Adyen, Stripe, Checkout.com, DBS, OCBC and UOB | **REAL** | Same release, list of acceptance partners |
| 3 | Over 100 issuers in 10 markets joined the first phase since April 2026 | **REAL (their figure)** | Same release |
| 4 | AI agents can now pay at these merchants for you | **OUR READ** | Not yet. Partners will "assess their existing systems", "test agent-initiated Visa transactions" and "identify gaps". The release announces no consumer launch date |
| 5 | Visa is piloting stablecoin prefunding for cross-border payouts in Asia Pacific, with dtcpay | **REAL** | [Visa release](https://www.visa.com.sg/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases/visa-direct-advances-stablecoin-powered-money-movement-in-asia-pacific.html), Nov 18: dtcpay, a Singapore payments company, "will use supported stablecoins to prefund cross-border fiat payouts through Visa Direct" |
| 6 | People now get paid in stablecoins through Visa | **NOT CLAIMED** | The business funds with stablecoins. Recipients "receive funds in fiat currency" |
| 7 | The same prefunding has been commercial in the US since October 2026, with Inyo Global | **REAL (their statement)** | Same release |
| 8 | Visa Direct reaches more than 18 billion endpoints | **REAL (their figure)** | Same release |

## Why it matters

Agent checkout needs the shop's side to work, not only the bank card. This phase is about exactly that, which is why the list is acquirers and gateways rather than consumers. Stablecoins here sit behind the curtain: they move a company's money to Visa faster, and the person on the other end still sees dollars, euros or pesos.
