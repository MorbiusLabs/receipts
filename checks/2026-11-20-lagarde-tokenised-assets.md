# Lagarde: tokenised assets are €23 billion and five times bigger in a year. The digital euro is aimed at 2029

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-20-lagarde-tokenised-assets/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-20 · **Status:** all claims sourced

ECB President Christine Lagarde spoke at the Frankfurt European Banking Congress on Nov 20.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tokenised assets are worth about €23 billion, five times more than a year ago | **REAL (their figures)** | [ECB speech](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/key/date/2026/html/ecb.sp261120~e52bcc4db2.en.html): "worth around €23 billion worldwide in March, but it was five times its size a year earlier". The footnote limits it to tokenised traditional assets on public blockchains. €23B / 5 = about €4.6B a year earlier |
| 2 | The digital euro arrives in 2029 | **WATCH** | Conditional: "If the legislation is adopted by the end of this year, we aim to be ready for a first issuance in 2029." |
| 3 | A €1 billion bond settled on the ECB's new tokenised platform | **REAL** | Lagarde: "the European Stability Mechanism settled a €1 billion digitally native bond on Pontes". [Bundesbank release](https://www.bundesbank.de/en/press/press-releases/esm-is-first-european-supranational-issuer-to-settle-dlt-based-bond-issuance-on-eurosystem-s-pontes-platform-with-bundesbank-and-clearstream-1013002), Oct 26: "a first-time digital ESM benchmark bond of €1 billion" |
| 4 | Pontes settles tokenised trades in central bank money | **REAL** | Lagarde: it "allows tokenised transactions to settle in central bank money on Eurosystem infrastructure". Bundesbank: launched "on 21 September 2026" |
| 5 | Europe would need €1.3 trillion to triple its AI computing share | **REAL (their estimate)** | Lagarde cites an estimate: raising Europe's share of world AI computing capacity "from around 5% to 15% by 2030 would require investment of around €1.3 trillion" |
| 6 | Lagarde announced she is leaving the ECB | **NOT CLAIMED** | She said this is her last time speaking at this congress as ECB president: "it will sadly be the last." The speech announces no departure date |
