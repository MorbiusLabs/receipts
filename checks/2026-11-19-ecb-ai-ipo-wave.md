# The ECB says the AI IPO wave is heading past the dotcom peak, and names Anthropic

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-19-ecb-ai-ipo-wave/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-19 · **Status:** all claims sourced

The European Central Bank published its November 2026 Financial Stability Review on Nov 18, the day Anthropic priced. It is a risk assessment, not a market call. Here is what it says about AI, IPOs and stablecoins, word for word.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The AI-driven IPO surge is on track to pass the dotcom bubble peak as a share of US GDP | **REAL (their analysis)** | [ECB Financial Stability Review, Nov 2026](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/financial-stability-publications/fsr/html/ecb.fsr202611~e03b919d36.en.html): "This jump in issuance is now on track to surpass the dotcom bubble peak as a share of US GDP. Historically, such trends have often coincided with market peaks, signalling late-cycle exuberance" |
| 2 | The review names Anthropic and OpenAI | **REAL** | Special Feature C: "SpaceX’s record initial public offering (IPO) has led the way, with OpenAI and Anthropic planning to follow" |
| 3 | The ECB predicts a crash, or calls Anthropic's price too high | **NOT CLAIMED** | The review says large IPOs "could be a sign of late-cycle market exuberance" and could add volatility. It says nothing about Anthropic's price or valuation |
| 4 | AI infrastructure is increasingly funded with debt, which could carry losses into bond markets | **REAL (their analysis)** | [ECB press release](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2026/html/ecb.pr261118~83c5c60158.en.html), Nov 18: "a potential change in sentiment could spread losses from equity markets to bond markets" |
| 5 | Free cash flow turned negative for most US hyperscalers in 2026 | **REAL (their analysis)** | Review, Box 3: "free cash flows turned negative for most US hyperscalers in 2026, as capital expenditure on AI infrastructure exceeded operating cash flows" |
| 6 | US big tech AI capex is projected at $5.5 trillion by 2030 | **REAL (their figure)** | Review: "projected to total USD 5.5 trillion by 2030" |
| 7 | Euro area banks can take the hit | **REAL (their analysis)** | Vice-President Boris Vujčić: "Banks are well placed to withstand shocks, while vulnerabilities are more pronounced in certain non-banks" |
| 8 | The ECB suggests easing one stablecoin rule under MiCA | **REAL** | Review: minimum deposit requirements for stablecoins "could be removed and replaced with minimum liquidity requirements for reserve assets" |

## Not on this page

The review shows the dotcom comparison in a chart (Chart 2.8). We do not print a percentage of GDP until we have the chart's data.
