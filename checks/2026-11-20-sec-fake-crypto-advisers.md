# SEC: eight fake advisers and "exchanges" took $37 million, with forged SEC certificates

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-20-sec-fake-crypto-advisers/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-20 · **Status:** all claims sourced; these are the SEC's allegations, not court findings

On Nov 19 the SEC sued eight Colorado-based entities it says were fronts for a scam run from overseas. Victims were shown fake SEC certificates to make them trust the platforms.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The SEC charged eight entities with taking more than $37 million from US investors | **REAL** | [SEC release 2026-115](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-115-sec-eight-entities-used-fake-crypto-asset-trading-platforms-steal-more-37-million-us-investors), Nov 19: "eight Colorado-based entities, likely operated by individuals located overseas, for defrauding U.S. investors out of more than $37 million" |
| 2 | The scammers showed doubters fake SEC certificates | **REAL (SEC's allegation)** | Release: "making fake SEC certificates, making filings with the SEC, FINRA, or FinCEN, and directing skeptical investors to those certificates and filings" |
| 3 | It started with social media ads and group chats, then stock tips, then a crypto platform | **REAL (SEC's allegation)** | Release: ads led to "group and private chats", where victims first got advice "to trade stocks on U.S. markets" before being pushed to send money to "a supposed crypto asset trading platform" |
| 4 | It ran for almost two years under changing names | **REAL (SEC's allegation)** | Release: "four adviser-platform pairs from August 2024 through May 2026", each abandoned "after operating for a few months" |
| 5 | A filing on the SEC's website means a firm is legitimate | **FALSE** | The scheme itself used real filings. The SEC says it removed a Form D and two Forms ADV filed by three of the defendants. It tells investors to check people on [Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/) |
| 6 | The defendants have been found guilty | **FALSE** | This is a civil complaint in the US District Court for the District of Colorado. It seeks injunctions, disgorgement, interest and penalties. No court has ruled |
| 7 | AI was used in the scam | **NOT CLAIMED** | The release does not mention AI |
