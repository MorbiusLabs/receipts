# AI agents checking out through Adyen can now pay with Klarna, the first option there that is not a card

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-21-klarna-adyen-agentic/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-21 · **Status:** all claims sourced to the companies' own release; no usage figures exist yet

Klarna and Adyen announced the launch on Nov 20, 2026 in a joint release on Business Wire, marked "This is a paid press release" on [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/adyen-merchants-now-offer-klarna-133000819.html). It is the companies' own claim. What it does not say matters as much as what it does.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shoppers can pay with Klarna when an AI agent checks out for them through Adyen | **REAL (their claim)** | Release: shoppers "can now pay with Klarna when an AI agent checks out for them through Adyen Agentic." Dated New York, Nov 20, 2026 |
| 2 | Klarna is the first non-card payment option in Adyen's agent checkout | **REAL (their claim)** | Release: "Klarna is the first payment option beyond cards available in Adyen Agentic, Adyen's agentic commerce suite" |
| 3 | Shoppers still confirm each purchase | **REAL (their claim)** | Release: "Shoppers review and confirm every purchase before it is completed." The agent shows the options the merchant already offers, including "interest-free installments or with financing" |
| 4 | It is live in specific countries or at named merchants | **NOT CLAIMED** | The release names no countries, merchants, AI platforms or volumes. It says merchants that already offer Klarna through Adyen "can switch it on with no new integration work" |
| 5 | It adds a credit decision to agent checkout | **OUR READ** | Installments and financing are credit products. With this option, an agent's checkout can end in a pay-later plan, not only a card charge, while the shopper confirms it |
