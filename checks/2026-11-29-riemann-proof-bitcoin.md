# AI helped prove the Riemann hypothesis, and machines checked it. It did not win the $1M prize, and it does not break Bitcoin

**Post:** [X, Nov 29](https://x.com/morbius_labs/status/2126743975876812993) and [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-29-riemann-proof-bitcoin/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-29 · **Status:** sourced to the Lean FRO and Mathlib joint statement (Nov 21), the Clay Institute rules and the Ethereum Foundation blog (Nov 23); K33 and Chamath Palihapitiya via news reports

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | An AI-assisted proof of the Riemann hypothesis was released | **REAL** | [Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative](https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/riemann-hypothesis/), Nov 21: "On November 20, 2026, Levent Alpöge released a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis as a Lean library comprising 17 million lines of code." [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/think/news/anthropic-claude-riemann-hypothesis-math-proof): the Anthropic mathematician says "he used Claude" |
| 2 | The proof has been verified | **REAL** | Same statement: "We have full confidence that Alpöge's proof of the Riemann Hypothesis is correct". Checked by "Lean's official kernel" and "several independent checkers" |
| 3 | There is a proof mathematicians can read | **FALSE** | Not yet. The statement looks forward to work that "will help explain the new ideas in this proof". The check was by machine |
| 4 | AI won the $1 million Millennium Prize | **FALSE** | [Clay rules](https://www.claymath.org/millennium-problems/rules/): a solution must be published in a qualifying outlet, "at least two years must have passed since publication", and it needs "general acceptance in the global mathematics community" |
| 5 | Chamath Palihapitiya warned the math could threaten Bitcoin | **REAL (their reporting)** | [Stocktwits via TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/stocktwits:bd13a03a3094b:0-chamath-palihapitiya-warns-ai-s-alien-mathematics-could-break-bitcoin-as-ai-tackles-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis/): "This is a period of alien mathematics." and "nothing stands behind Bitcoin except the code that checks it". A risk he raises, not evidence of a break |
| 6 | The proof breaks Bitcoin's cryptography | **FALSE** | [K33, as reported by Bloomingbit](https://en.bloomingbit.io/feed/news/125419), Nov 28: a proof of the Riemann hypothesis "does not provide a way to derive a private key from a public key". Bitcoin signs with elliptic curves (secp256k1) |
| 7 | AI is now a bigger risk to crypto than quantum computers | **WATCH** | [Ethereum Foundation](https://blog.ethereum.org/en/2026/11/23/pqai-0), Nov 23: "recently AI emerged as a more urgent risk". The EF "has doubled the size of its protocol security team" |
| 8 | The Ethereum Foundation says the proof breaks cryptography | **FALSE** | Same post: it "does not immediately imply that AI can or will break the hardness assumptions that our cryptography relies on" |

## What would change this page

A human-readable proof, a journal publication, or any working method that recovers a private key from a public key.
