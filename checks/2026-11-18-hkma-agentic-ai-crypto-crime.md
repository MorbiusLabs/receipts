# Hong Kong's central bank wants AI agents to watch crypto crime. It is still exploring

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-18-hkma-agentic-ai-crypto-crime/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-18 · **Status:** all claims sourced

On Nov 17, Raymond Chan, Executive Director (Enforcement and AML) at the Hong Kong Monetary Authority, gave a keynote at an HKMA workshop on regtech for digital assets. Headlines read it as Hong Kong turning to AI agents against crypto crime. Here is what the speech says, and what stage it describes.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The HKMA is exploring agentic AI to help banks monitor crypto and fiat flows for financial crime | **REAL** | [HKMA speech](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/speeches/2026/11/20261117-1/), Nov 17: "We are therefore exploring the development of agentic A.I.", able to analyse "both on-chain transfers and off-chain customer transactions" |
| 2 | The HKMA has deployed AI agents that police crypto today | **NOT CLAIMED** | The speech says "exploring". Only decentralised identity tools are described as "being explored and piloted" |
| 3 | The target: fast layering between crypto and fiat | **REAL** | Same speech: flag anomalies "like rapid fund layering through switching between cryptos and fiat currencies" |
| 4 | Illicit crypto addresses received over $150 billion in 2025, 84% of it in stablecoins | **REAL (their reporting)** | Same speech, citing the 2026 Chainalysis Crypto Crime Report. We did not recompute it |
| 5 | Hong Kong's surveillance framework for stablecoins is only starting | **REAL** | Same speech: "we are just beginning to operationalise this surveillance framework" |
| 6 | Hong Kong runs a public-private crypto crime taskforce, VAIT | **REAL** | Same speech: it connects the Police, Customs, the HKMA, the SFC, licensed virtual asset platforms and stablecoin issuers |
| 7 | "No single tool offers complete protection" | **REAL** | Exact quote from the speech |

## Why it matters

Hong Kong licenses stablecoin issuers and crypto platforms. When its banking regulator says it wants AI that reads blockchain transfers and bank records together, it is describing what banks there may be asked to run. For now it is a plan, not a system.
