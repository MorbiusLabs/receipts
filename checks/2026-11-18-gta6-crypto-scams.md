# GTA 6 for 1 SOL: the scams that arrived before the game

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-18-gta6-crypto-scams/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-18 · **Status:** all claims sourced

Grand Theft Auto VI comes out on Nov 19. Fake stores, fake downloads and fake coins have been waiting for it for months, and several of them go after crypto wallets. We checked the claims you are most likely to see this week.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | GTA 6 launches on Nov 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and Series S | **REAL** | [Rockstar Games](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI), official game page, checked Nov 18 |
| 2 | A leaked or early PC build of GTA 6 is available to download | **FALSE** | Rockstar lists only PS5 and Xbox Series X and Series S. [Cynet](https://www.cynet.com/blog/download-gta6-for-free-inside-the-malware-trap/), Nov 13: "there is no PC version of the game included in the November 19 release" |
| 3 | A fake GTA 6 store priced at 1 SOL asks buyers to sign away almost their whole wallet | **REAL (their analysis)** | [Malwarebytes](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/scams/2026/09/fake-gta-6-leaked-copy-drains-your-crypto-wallet), Sep 1: the script "checks the wallet’s balance, leaves a small amount to cover the transaction fee, and prepares to transfer everything else to the attacker. The advertised price plays no part in the calculation." |
| 4 | A "free GTA 6 download" promoted through Google Colab is a password and wallet stealer | **REAL (their analysis)** | [Cynet](https://www.cynet.com/blog/download-gta6-for-free-inside-the-malware-trap/), Nov 13: "This file is not GTA 6", a WARDEN Stealer loader that takes passwords, cookies, Steam and Roblox logins and crypto wallet data, and "replaces copied cryptocurrency addresses". The page promised 72 MB; the file was a 796 KB executable |

We will check what actually shows up |

## What to do

- Buy only from PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store or a known retailer.
- A game shop never needs your wallet. If a site asks you to connect one, close it.
- Read the wallet approval screen. Reject anything that moves close to your whole balance, or asks for ongoing access to your tokens or NFTs.
