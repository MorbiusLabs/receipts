# Wells Fargo from Nov 24: what your AI agent does is "deemed" done by you, unless the law says otherwise

**Post:** [X, Nov 25](https://x.com/morbius_labs/status/2125620973101539653) and [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-21-wells-fargo-ai-agent-terms/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-21 · **Status:** all claims sourced to the agreement text

Wells Fargo has published a new Online Access Agreement that takes effect on Nov 24, 2026. It adds Section 15, "Third-Party Artificial Intelligence Agents or Agentic AI". Citi added a similar section this month ([our check](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-19-citi-ai-agent-authorized/)).

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | From Nov 24, whatever your AI agent does with your login counts as done by you | **REAL** | [Upcoming agreement](https://www.wellsfargo.com/online-banking/online-access-agreement/upcoming/), "Version Effective November 24, 2026", 15(d): if you give an agent your login or otherwise authorize it, its instructions, actions and transactions "are deemed to have been initiated and authorized directly by you, without further verification" |
| 2 | This is new | **REAL** | The [current agreement](https://www.wellsfargo.com/online-banking/online-access-agreement/), "Version Effective May 21, 2026", does not mention AI agents. "AI Agent" appears 45 times in the Nov 24 text |
| 3 | That includes an agent that was tricked or went beyond what you allowed | **REAL** | 15(e): you are responsible for "unintended actions performed by the AI Agent, actions resulting from incomplete, inaccurate, or manipulated inputs, or the AI Agent exceeding the authority granted by you." Agent use is "AT YOUR OWN RISK" |
| 4 | The bank watches what agents do | **FALSE** | 15(e): "we do not monitor, screen, or review AI Agent activities, including any transactions initiated by an AI Agent." |
| 5 | You stay on the hook until you tell the bank you switched the agent off | **REAL** | 15(f): "You must retain evidence of such revocation and must provide such evidence to us, at our request." Until you notify the bank, "you remain responsible for all the AI Agent’s activities, to the extent permitted by law or regulation." |
| 6 | Customers lose their federal protections | **NOT CLAIMED** | Section 15(d) opens with "Unless otherwise provided by law or regulation", and 15(f) ends with "to the extent permitted by law or regulation". The contract does not say it overrides the law. How federal rules on unauthorized transfers apply to agents has not been tested |
| 7 | Wells Fargo now lets customers use AI agents | **NOT CLAIMED** | The agreement lists "Your responsibilities for artificial intelligence agents, if and when we permit the use of such agents." 15(g) lets the bank require that agents be registered, or block them |
| 8 | Banks are moving together on this | **OUR READ** | Citi and Wells Fargo now use similar language ("authorized by You", "deemed ... authorized directly by you"). No source we read says the banks coordinated |

## What would change this page

A Wells Fargo statement, a revised version before Nov 24, or a CFPB view on how unauthorized-transfer rules apply to AI agents.

Update 2026-11-24: the terms are now in effect. The [main agreement page](https://www.wellsfargo.com/online-banking/online-access-agreement/) shows "Version Effective November 24, 2026", with Section 15 and the same "deemed to have been initiated and authorized directly by you" wording.
