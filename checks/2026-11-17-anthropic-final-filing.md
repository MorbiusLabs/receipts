# Anthropic's last filing before pricing: range unchanged, Nasdaq approved

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-17-anthropic-final-filing/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-17 · **Status:** all claims sourced

Anthropic prices its IPO on Nov 18. On the morning of Nov 17 it filed one more amendment with the SEC. We read the cover page and compared it with the Nov 10 version.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Anthropic filed a third amended S-1 on Nov 17, accepted at 11:26 UTC | **REAL** | [EDGAR filing index for Anthropic, PBC](https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0002130357.json): S-1/A, accession 0001628280-26-074338. An 8-A12B exchange registration followed at 11:49 UTC |
| 2 | The price range is still $70 to $78 | **REAL** | [S-1/A No. 3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074338/anthropicpbc-sx1ax3.htm): "the initial public offering price per share will be between $70.00 and $78.00" |
| 3 | Nasdaq has approved the listing under ANT | **REAL** | Same filing: "We have been approved to list our Class A common stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol “ANT.”" The Nov 10 version said the company had applied |
| 4 | 825 million shares: 801.35 million from Anthropic, 23.65 million from existing holders | **REAL** | Same filing: "We are selling 801,351,354 shares ... the selling stockholders ... are selling an additional 23,648,646 shares". 801,351,354 + 23,648,646 = 825,000,000 |
| 5 | Underwriters can buy 123.75 million more shares, 15% of the deal | **REAL** | Same filing: "the option to purchase up to an additional 123,750,000 shares". 123.75 / 825 = 15% |
| 6 | Strategic partners will buy $26.0 billion of non-voting Class N stock at the IPO price | **REAL** | Same filing: "an aggregate of $26.0 billion of our Class N common stock in a private placement at a price per share equal to the initial public offering price". This is separate from the IPO itself |
| 7 | Bankers are weighing a price above the range | **WATCH** | Reported by the Financial Times, as cited by [Finimize](https://finimize.com/content/anthropics-bankers-weigh-pricing-its-ipo-above-range). Not in any filing. A company can price outside its filed range without a new amendment, so the filing does not rule it out |
