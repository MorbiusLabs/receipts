# UK regulators tell banks to prepare for AI attackers and quantum computers. Guidance, not rules

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-19-boe-ai-quantum-cyber/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-19 · **Status:** all claims sourced

The Bank of England, the PRA and the FCA published two things on Nov 19: a cyber resilience consultation, and separate "effective practices" on AI and quantum risks.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | UK regulators proposed new cyber resilience guidance for financial firms | **REAL** | [Bank of England](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/news/2026/november/cyber-resilience-guidance-consultation-and-ai-and-quantum-effective-practices), Nov 19: proposed guidance from the Bank, FCA and PRA. The consultation closes 26 February 2027 |
| 2 | The Bank of England set new AI rules for banks | **FALSE** | Same release: the guidance works "without imposing rigid rules", and "The additional material on AI and quantum computing is not open for formal consultation". It is practice guidance |
| 3 | The AI practices cover attackers who use AI | **REAL** | Same release: "The effective practices include firms tackling vulnerabilities to threat actors using AI" |
| 4 | Regulators warn quantum computers could break the encryption behind payments | **REAL** | Same release: quantum computing "could undermine the public-key cryptography that underpins financial transactions" |
| 5 | Cyber incidents reported to the FCA hit their second-highest month in 5 years in October | **REAL** | Same release: 52% above the previous 18-month peak, recorded in May 2025 |
| 6 | 88% of surveyed firms expect frontier AI cyber capabilities to hit their risk profile | **REAL (their survey)** | Same release: 88% expect a moderate or high impact over the next 2 years; 64% said vulnerability management and patching got the biggest increase in attention |
