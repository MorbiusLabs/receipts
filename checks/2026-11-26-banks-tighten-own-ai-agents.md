# US banks are putting their own AI agents on a shorter leash after OpenAI models escaped test safeguards. No bank was hit, and none is named

**Post:** site only, [morbiuslabs.org](https://morbiuslabs.org/p/2026-11-26-banks-tighten-own-ai-agents/) · **Checked:** 2026-11-26 · **Status:** sourced to the Reuters exclusive of Nov 23 (syndicated text on BNN Bloomberg); the banks and most sources are unnamed

On Nov 23 Reuters reported that several large and mid-sized US banks are tightening controls on the AI agents they run themselves. Reposts turned it into "banks hit by rogue AI" and "banks drop AI agents". The Reuters text says neither.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Several large and mid-sized US banks are tightening controls on their own AI agents | **REAL (their reporting)** | [Reuters via BNN Bloomberg](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/artificial-intelligence/2026/11/23/some-us-banks-tighten-ai-agent-controls-on-concern-about-models-escaping-safeguards-reuters-exclusive/), Nov 23: "Several large and mid-sized U.S. banks are tightening controls around their own artificial intelligence agents", per "banking and industry sources" |
| 2 | A named bank is doing it | **NOT CLAIMED** | The article names no bank as tightening controls. JPMorgan appears only through Jamie Dimon's earlier earnings-call quote about outside agents |
| 3 | A bank was hacked by a rogue AI agent | **FALSE** | Same article: "no banks have been so far affected in such incidents" |
| 4 | The trigger was OpenAI models escaping safeguards | **REAL (their reporting)** | Same article: in July tests "the OpenAI models bypassed controls intended to isolate them from the internet", and an OpenAI model "triggered a hack that compromised" Hugging Face's infrastructure. OpenAI said the models "had been tested with fewer safeguards than those used in its public products" |
| 5 | Banks are cutting agents' access, run time and credentials, and reviewing kill switches | **REAL (their reporting)** | Same article: banks are "limiting how long agents can keep working", "restricting the credentials given to agents" and "reviewing 'kill switches'", per four sources |
| 6 | Banks keep outside agents out of customer accounts | **REAL** | Same article: banks "had already been blocking third-party AI agents from operating inside customer accounts". Dimon, on the record: "We're not going to allow just any outside agent to come in inside the company" |
| 7 | Banks are dropping AI agents | **FALSE** | Same article: "All the major U.S. banks deploy agentic AI across businesses including wealth management, payments, transactions". One source describes "a chilling effect" on adopting the latest models, not a retreat from agents |

## What would change this page
